How we handle supplier invoices today (written by Finance)

All supplier invoices are supposed to come to invoice@newworks.dk. Some still go to people directly and get forwarded.

1. Every morning we go through the mailbox and open each mail. Most have a PDF, some have the invoice in the mail text.
   Reminders and statements also land here, and sometimes receipts from colleagues.
2. We check that the supplier exists in Business Central. If not, we create it.
3. The PDF goes into our document capture tool. It reads some fields, but we type or correct most of it:
   supplier, invoice number, dates, amounts, currency. Then we choose G/L account, VAT code and cost centre ourselves.
4. For goods we look up the purchase order in BC and compare price and quantity by hand.
5. We email the PDF to the person who has to approve it. Then we wait, and chase them if nothing happens.
   Approvals sit in our inboxes, so it is hard to see what is waiting for whom.
6. When it is approved we post the invoice in BC.
7. Twice a week we pay due invoices in the netbank. Foreign currency payments are done one by one.

What goes wrong: duplicates we only catch when the supplier says they were paid twice, invoices nobody approves,
VAT coded wrong on foreign invoices, and late payment because the invoice sat in someone's mail.
